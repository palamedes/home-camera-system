Setting a shade's upper and lower stop limits The remote does this, not Sentry — open when a shade stops in the wrong place

Every motor stores its own upper and lower stop limit, in the motor itself. Sentry's 0–100% scale is relative to those limits, per shade: 0% is that shade's upper limit and 100% is its lower limit. That is why one command moves them all correctly despite different window ledges — the calibration lives in each motor, so Sentry never needs a per-shade offset.

Before you start

  • On a multi-channel remote, select that shade's channel first. Channel 0 is the group channel and would retune everything.
  • In program mode the shade only creeps. Hold UP or DOWN to make it travel continuously.
  • A "jog" is a quick little up-and-down twitch. It is the motor acknowledging you — if you never see one, nothing after that will work.

Lower limit — the ledge case

  1. Press DOWN. The shade runs to its current lower limit.
  2. Press and hold DOWN + STOP together until it jogs once, then beeps twice. You are now in program mode.
  3. Move the shade to where you actually want it to stop.
  4. Press and hold DOWN + STOP again until it jogs twice and beeps once. Saved.

Upper limit

  1. Press UP to run to the current upper limit.
  2. Press and hold UP + STOP until it jogs once, then beeps twice.
  3. Move the shade to the desired top.
  4. Press and hold UP + STOP until it jogs twice and beeps once.

Middle stop limit

A third preset. Move the shade where you want it, then press and hold STOP for 5 seconds until it jogs once and beeps once. Afterwards, pressing STOP while the shade is not moving sends it to that middle position. Both the upper and lower limits must be set first.

Pairing a new shade to the hub

P2 is a recessed button on the back of the remote, under the battery cover, on the left. Slide the cover off, select the shade's channel, then in the vendor app choose Add Device → Radio Motor → your hub → By Remote, press P2 twice (shade jogs twice), then Next (shade jogs three times). Pairing is the most range-sensitive thing these do — if it fails, move the hub closer before anything else.

Do not do this by accident

Holding the setting button on the motor head until the shade jogs four times and beeps three times is a full reset. It wipes both stop limits, and Sentry's percentages become meaningless until you re-set them — upper first, then lower.
